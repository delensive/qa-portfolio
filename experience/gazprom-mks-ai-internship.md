## About the Project

Participated in an AI project focused on improving industrial safety through computer vision technologies.

The goal of the project was to prepare high-quality datasets for training machine learning models capable of detecting safety violations at industrial facilities.

---

## Responsibilities

- Annotated images and video frames for computer vision models.
- Classified safety-related events according to project guidelines.
- Detected violations such as missing personal protective equipment (PPE), unsafe behavior and restricted area violations.
- Verified annotation quality and consistency before submission.
- Worked with large datasets containing surveillance camera footage.
- Followed annotation standards and internal quality requirements.
- Contributed to improving the quality of training datasets for AI models.
